The Preacher's Craft.

Exegesis, structure, and delivery — the pastoral craft behind sermons that honor Scripture and speak to the people in front of you.

4 Lessons 3h 04m Runtime ★ 4.9 (2.1k)
Watch free preview Subscribe

About this course

Across 12 lessons, David deconstructs the craft of preaching sermons that honor Scripture and speak to the people in front of you — from meeting the text on its own terms, to building a sermon that lands, to standing in front of a congregation with clarity and pastoral weight. You'll leave with a sermon-preparation practice, not a checklist.

Lessons

  1. Lesson 01 Free preview

    Introduction: The Weight of the Word

    What it means to preach the Word, and why the way we prepare has to answer to that weight first.

    12 min
  2. Lesson 02

    Exegesis: Meeting the Text on Its Terms

    A working method for sitting with a passage before you ever start writing.

    24 min
  3. Lesson 03

    Structure: Building a Sermon That Lands

    Frameworks that get out of the way of the message, and when to break them.

    31 min
  4. Lesson 04

    Delivery: Voice, Pause, and Presence

    Standing in the pulpit as a pastor first, a speaker second.

    28 min
Your instructor

David Kim has served as the lead teaching pastor at Grace Community Church for fifteen years. In this class, he opens the sermon-preparation practice he built through a decade of pulpit ministry — from meeting the text to standing in front of a congregation.
